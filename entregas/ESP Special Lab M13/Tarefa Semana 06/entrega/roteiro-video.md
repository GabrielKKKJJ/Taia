## Script

Hi, my name is Djordan Moura, and in this video I want to talk about two certifications
from the article that I would really like to take, and why I chose them.

The first one is the **AWS Certified Developer – Associate**. I chose it because almost
everything I am building right now ends up running in the cloud. This term, in my
Programming and Architecture courses, I have been working with a .NET backend, an Angular
frontend, and real-time protocols like SignalR and MQTT. All of that eventually needs to
be deployed, scaled, and monitored somewhere. AWS is still the leading cloud platform, so
this certification would validate skills I am already practising, instead of teaching me
something disconnected from my daily work. It would also force me to understand things I
tend to avoid, like permissions, deployment pipelines, and cost.

The second one is the **Certified Secure Software Lifecycle Professional**, the CSSLP. I
picked this one for a different reason. In my Internet of Things course, I keep seeing how
easy it is to build a device that works perfectly and is completely insecure. Security is
usually the part we add at the end, when it is already too late. The CSSLP focuses on
putting security into every phase of the lifecycle, not just at the end, and I think that
mindset is what separates a student project from professional software.

So, one certification to strengthen what I already do, and one to fix a habit I want to
change. Between the two, I would start with the AWS one, because it is closer to my
current level, and then move to the CSSLP once I have more industry experience.

Thank you for watching.
